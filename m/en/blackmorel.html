Black morel

Morchella importuna · 黑脉羊肚菌
Rare羊肚菌科🔥 Edible With Preparation

Sources report it requires expert processing before it can be eaten; cases of poisoning from improper handling have been recorded.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap dark brown to black, with vertical ridges like a ladder
  • Cap fused to the stem, entirely hollow inside
  • Grows in spring on wood chips, flower beds, and urban green spaces

Fun Fact

This is the leading species grown in China's field-cultivated morel industry; 'black-veined' refers to the vertical ridges darkening as it matures.
