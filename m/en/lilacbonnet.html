Lilac bonnet

Mycena pura · 洁小菇
Occasional小菇科⚠️ Poisonous

Reference sources list this as a poisonous mushroom.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap pinkish purple to pale purple, bell-shaped, margin striate
  • Gills pale purple; stem the same color and hollow
  • Smells strongly of radish; grows in leaf litter

Fun Fact

It has a distinct radish smell, contains trace muscarine, and has even been reported to carry hallucinogenic compounds — one of the few Mycena species clearly documented as toxic.
