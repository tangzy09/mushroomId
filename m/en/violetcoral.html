Violet coral

Clavaria zollingeri · 紫珊瑚菌
Rarely Seen珊瑚菌科❓ Edibility Unknown

Edibility is unknown and should be treated as poisonous.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Entirely purple, with coral-like branches
  • Brittle in texture; grows in grassland and woodland
  • Vividly colored, standing out against green grass

Fun Fact

An indicator of healthy grassland, it only appears where the soil has gone untilled and unfertilized for years, and it's listed on the Red List in several European countries.
