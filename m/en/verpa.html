Early morel

Verpa bohemica · 皱盖钟菌
Occasional羊肚菌科⚠️ Poisonous

Reference sources list this as a poisonous mushroom.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap longitudinally wrinkled, attached to the stem only at the top, thimble-like
  • Stem white, stuffed with cottony filling inside
  • Appears in early spring, ahead of true morels

Fun Fact

It fruits earlier in the season than true morels, its cap attached to the stem only at the very top, like a bell hanging from a hook; some people experience coordination problems after eating it.
