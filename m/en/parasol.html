Parasol mushroom

Macrolepiota procera · 高大环柄菇
Common蘑菇科🔥 Edible With Preparation

Sources report it requires expert processing before it can be eaten; cases of poisoning from improper handling have been recorded.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap large and flat, with brown scales arranged in concentric rings
  • Stem slender, patterned like snakeskin
  • Ring thick and movable up and down the stem

Fun Fact

Its stem carries a snakeskin-like brown pattern, and the ring can slide up and down; the green-spored parasol it's often confused with has, as the name suggests, a green spore print.
