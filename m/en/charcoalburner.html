Charcoal burner

Russula cyanoxantha · 蓝黄红菇
Occasional红菇科🍽 Reported Edible (Wild)

Reference sources describe this as an edible wild fungus. This guide provides no basis whatsoever for foraging or consumption.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap color variable, often a mix of purple, green, and blue-gray
  • Gills white and pliable, not crumbling when rubbed, unlike other Russula
  • Stem white; grows in hardwood forests

Fun Fact

Most Russula gills crumble at a touch, but this one is pliable enough to bend like oiled paper without snapping — its best field mark.
