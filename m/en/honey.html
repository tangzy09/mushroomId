Honey fungus

Armillaria mellea · 蜜环菌
Common膨瑚菌科🔥 Edible With Preparation

Sources report it requires expert processing before it can be eaten; cases of poisoning from improper handling have been recorded.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap honey-yellow to brown, with fine scales at the center
  • Stem with a prominent white membranous ring
  • Grows in large clusters at tree bases and roots, often in extensive patches

Fun Fact

Its black root-like cords, called rhizomorphs, glow faintly green at night — one source behind old legends of 'foxfire' in the woods.
