Tinder fungus

Fomes fomentarius · 木蹄层孔菌
Common多孔菌科❓ Inedible

Non-toxic, but woody or intensely bitter in texture — not eaten as food.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Hoof-shaped, grayish brown with concentric zones
  • Woody in texture, perennial, adding one layer each year
  • Grows on living or dead birch and beech

Fun Fact

Otzi the Iceman, who lived 5,300 years ago, carried this fungus with him; its processed flesh could hold a smoldering ember — one of the oldest known pieces of evidence for humans using fungi.
