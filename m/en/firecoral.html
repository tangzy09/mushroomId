Poison fire coral

Trichoderma cornu-damae · 火焰茸
Rare肉座菌科☠️ Deadly Poisonous

Reference sources describe this as highly toxic, with recorded fatalities.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Bright red to orange-red, finger- or antler-like branches
  • Emerges from the ground or rotten wood, hard in texture
  • Found in Japan, Korea, and southern China; even contact can cause skin irritation

Fun Fact

It contains trichothecene toxins and is one of the rare fungi reported to cause skin reactions from mere contact; accidental ingestion carries a high death rate.
