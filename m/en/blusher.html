The blusher

Amanita rubescens · 赭盖鹅膏
Common鹅膏科🔥 Edible With Preparation

Sources report it requires expert processing before it can be eaten; cases of poisoning from improper handling have been recorded.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap reddish brown, with grayish white wart-like scales
  • Bruised or insect-damaged spots slowly turn red, the source of its name
  • Stem has a ring; base swollen but volva indistinct

Panther capThe blusher reddens when damaged and lacks the rim-like volva band at the base; panther cap does not change colour.Fly agaricThe blusher has a brownish-red cap that reddens when damaged; its warts rub off easily and are not neatly arranged.

Fun Fact

Spots nibbled by insects or bruised by a touch slowly flush wine-red, which is how it got its name; raw specimens contain a blood-cell-destroying toxin, and records say it must be cooked thoroughly.
