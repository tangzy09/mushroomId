Small stagshorn

Calocera cornea · 小孢绿杯珊瑚
Occasional花耳科❓ Inedible

Non-toxic, but woody or intensely bitter in texture — not eaten as food.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Small, yellow, horn-shaped, unbranched or sparsely branched
  • Gelatinous, only one to two centimeters tall
  • Grows in groups on dead hardwood
