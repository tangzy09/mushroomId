Horse mushroom

Agaricus arvensis · 野蘑菇
Occasional蘑菇科🍽 Reported Edible (Wild)

Reference sources describe this as an edible wild fungus. This guide provides no basis whatsoever for foraging or consumption.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap large and white, staining faintly yellow when bruised
  • Gills grayish pink, darkening to deep brown; ring double-layered, the lower layer gear-like
  • Smells sweetly of anise; grows in pastures and meadows

Fun Fact

It has a distinct almond or anise scent, a sharp contrast to the yellow stainer's inky smell — though scent alone should never be the deciding test.
