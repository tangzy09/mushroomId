Death cap

Amanita phalloides · 毒鹅膏
Occasional鹅膏科☠️ Deadly Poisonous

Reference sources describe this as highly toxic, with recorded fatalities.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Stem base has a white cup-shaped volva, often buried under leaf litter
  • Cap yellow-green to olive, faintly glossy when wet; margin not striate
  • Gills remain white; upper stem has a white membranous ring

Fun Fact

It accounts for more mushroom poisoning deaths worldwide than any other species. Its amatoxins survive cooking unchanged, and victims often go through a deceptive 1-2 day period of feeling better before symptoms return — a delay that can cost precious time before help is sought.
