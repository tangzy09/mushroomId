East Asian yellow death cap

Amanita subjunquillea · 黄盖鹅膏
Rarely Seen鹅膏科☠️ Deadly Poisonous

Reference sources describe this as highly toxic, with recorded fatalities.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap yellow to yellowish brown, darker at the center
  • Stem white, base with a white sac-like volva
  • Ring white and membranous, gills white; found in pine and hardwood forests

Fun Fact

It contains amatoxins and is a major cause of death in East Asia; its yellow-brown cap is easily mistaken for the edible Caesar's mushroom or egg mushroom.
