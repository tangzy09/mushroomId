Brick cap

Hypholoma lateritium · 砖红垂幕菇
Occasional球盖菇科❓ Edibility Unknown

Edibility is unknown and should be treated as poisonous.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap brick-red, darker at center, pale yellow at margin
  • Gills grayish white, aging to purplish brown; stem shows cortina remnants
  • Clusters on hardwood stumps in late autumn
