Panther cap

Amanita pantherina · 豹斑鹅膏
Occasional鹅膏科⚠️ Poisonous

Reference sources list this as a poisonous mushroom.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap brown, densely covered in neat rows of pure white warts
  • Stem base bulbous, with one to several ring-like volva edges above it
  • Ring white, gills white; cap margin finely striate

Fun Fact

A neurotoxic mushroom in the same category as fly agaric, and often more potent; its cap is brown with white spots, and the volva has a distinct collar-like rim.
