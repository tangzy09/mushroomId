Gypsy mushroom

Cortinarius caperatus · 皱盖丝膜菌
Occasional丝膜菌科🔥 Edible With Preparation

Sources report it requires expert processing before it can be eaten; cases of poisoning from improper handling have been recorded.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap ochre-yellow, coated in a frost-like powder
  • Stem with a membranous ring; gills pale brown, aging to rusty brown
  • Grows in mossy conifer forests and among bilberry shrubs

Fun Fact

Most Cortinarius species are inedible or outright deadly, but this is one of the few with a history of being eaten; Nordic foragers recognize it by the wrinkled cap and the white ring.
