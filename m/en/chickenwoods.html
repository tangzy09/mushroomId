Chicken of the woods

Laetiporus sulphureus · 硫磺菌
Common拟层孔菌科🔥 Edible With Preparation

Sources report it requires expert processing before it can be eaten; cases of poisoning from improper handling have been recorded.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Bright orange-yellow, fan-shaped in overlapping tiers, colored like egg yolk
  • Underside sulphur-yellow with fine pores
  • Grows on hardwood trunks, sometimes forming a large mass

Fun Fact

Named for its chicken-breast-like texture, but a good number of people get gastrointestinal reactions after eating it, and specimens growing on trees like yew call for particular caution.
