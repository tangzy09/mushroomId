Sulphur tuft

Hypholoma fasciculare · 簇生垂幕菇
Common球盖菇科⚠️ Poisonous

Reference sources list this as a poisonous mushroom.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Cap sulphur-yellow, faintly orange-brown at the center
  • Gills yellow at first, aging to grayish green then purplish black
  • Densely clustered on rotten wood and stumps

Easily Confused With

Honey fungusHoney fungus has white gills and a ring; sulphur tuft's gills go from yellow-green to purple-black and it has no true ring.Brick capBrick cap has a brick-red cap and grey gills; sulphur tuft is sulphur-yellow with yellow-green gills.Flowerpot parasolSulphur tuft grows in dense clumps on stumps with a smooth cap; the flowerpot parasol has a powdery, scaly surface and grows singly in potting soil.

Fun Fact

Its sulfur-yellow clusters can be found nearly year-round and taste intensely bitter; it's often mistaken for honey fungus and picked by mistake, making it a common cause of poisoning in Europe.
