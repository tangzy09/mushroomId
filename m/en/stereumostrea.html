False turkey tail

Stereum ostrea · 扇形韧革菌
Occasional韧革菌科❓ Inedible

Non-toxic, but woody or intensely bitter in texture — not eaten as food.

The same species can be described differently across regions and stages of maturity, and countless look-alike species cannot be told apart by eye. Never use the information on this page to judge whether a real mushroom is safe to eat.

How to Tell

  • Fan-shaped and thin, orange-brown with concentric zones, resembling turkey tail
  • Underside smooth with no pores, pale yellowish brown
  • Grows in overlapping tiers on fallen hardwood logs

Fun Fact

Its bands are wider and warmer in tone than turkey tail's, and the underside is likewise smooth with no pores; telling apart this trio of 'true and false turkey tails' is a classic beginner's exercise.
